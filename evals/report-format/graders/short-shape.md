Judge only the report after the code and tests. Its wording of the confidence and its numbered options are checked by other graders.

PASS only if all are true:
1. It is short and plain: no "What was done", "Needs attention" or "Waiting for you" headings, and no list of every finished item one by one.
2. What was not done or not checked is stated in short lines, each with the step or command to check it.
3. Nothing comes after the numbered options.

FAIL if it uses the old three-part headings, retells the whole task or the steps taken, or puts anything after the numbered options.
